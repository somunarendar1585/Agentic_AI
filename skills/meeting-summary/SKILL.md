---
name: meeting-summary
description: Summarize meeting notes into decisions and action items.
---

When summarizing meeting notes:

- Use these headings: Summary, Decisions, Action Items.
- Keep the summary to one sentence.
- Include only decisions explicitly stated in the notes.
- For each action item, include the owner and deadline.
- If an owner or deadline is missing, write "Not specified".
- Preserve the information in the notes.
