---
name: work-update
description: Turn rough work notes into a short professional update.
---

When writing a work update:

- Start with "Today's progress".
- Convert the supplied notes into clear bullet points.
- Use past tense for completed work.
- Preserve the meaning of the notes.
- Include only work mentioned in the notes.
- Keep the update under 80 words.
- Finish with "Ready for review."
